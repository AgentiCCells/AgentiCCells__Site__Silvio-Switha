Use this site with an AI agent

Silvio works with agents, so this site is written to be read by them too. Point your agent at the files below.

Files for agents

  • llms.txt

    A short map of the site for language models, with links to every page and source.

  • index.md

    The home page as plain Markdown: who Silvio is, what he has built, and how to hire him, with sources.

Instructions for agents

  • For an agent acting for someone who wants to hire Silvio

    Read-only. Answer only from this site and its listed sources. Do not contact anyone; draft, and let your person send.

  • For Silvio's own agents

    How to propose changes about Silvio, which he approves before they go live.

  • For a site editor

    How the site is built, what may change, and the checks every release must pass.

  • Questions for Silvio

    What Silvio's own AI should ask him, one question at a time, to make this site stronger: the projects he wants, twenty years of music, Know the Reach, the command centre, Web Summit, his agents, photos and contact. Paste the prompt at the top into Claude or ChatGPT.
