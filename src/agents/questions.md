# Questions for Silvio: what his AI should ask him, to make his site better

For Silvio's own AI (Claude, ChatGPT or his conductor). Many of the best facts are still in Silvio's head. Interview him one question at a time, write down his answers, and turn them into proposed changes he approves.

A prompt Silvio can paste into his AI:

> Read https://silvio-switha.agenticcells.site/agents/questions.md and https://silvio-switha.agenticcells.site/index.md. Then interview me for my website. Ask one question at a time, starting with section 1. After each section, show me what you would add or change on the site, in my words, and wait for my yes. Write my answers to interview-notes.md for me to keep. Portuguese or English, as I prefer.

## How to interview him

- One question at a time; ask for a story, then a number ("Can you give me one example?", "Roughly how many?").
- Use his words; offer a tidier version; he chooses.
- Mark each answer: public or private, and its source (a link, a file, a photo).
- After each section, propose the exact changes (which page, which sentence) and wait for his yes. Then follow https://silvio-switha.agenticcells.site/agents/silvio.md.

## 1. What he wants to be hired for

1. Which projects do you want most: command centres and dashboards, data visualisation, product videos, music, agent teams for start-ups, design? In what order?
2. Who is the ideal client: a start-up, a marketing team, a band or artist, an event, a company bringing in agents?
3. Where do you work: the Algarve, Lisbon, remote, abroad? Which languages?
4. How do you like to work with a client: a fixed project, a retainer, a sprint of a few days?
5. What can a client have from you in one week? In one month?
6. Should the site show prices or a "from" price? (Only if you want it public.)

## 2. Twenty years of music

1. When did you start, and on what instrument? When did you start singing and producing?
2. The wedding band: can the site name it? How many musicians, how many events a year, roughly, and since when?
3. What other bands, recordings or releases can the site mention?
4. What does a producer do that a musician does not? Give one example from Know the Reach.
5. Will you record Know the Reach with your band, as Dinis challenged you to?
6. Any live videos or recordings you would like on the site?

## 3. Know the Reach

1. Where did the idea for the song come from, and how long did the whole song take?
2. What did you change most between the first version and the final one?
3. Which shot in the video are you proudest of, and why?
4. Which tools did you use, beyond the ones in the production audit, and for what?
5. What would you do differently on the next one?
6. What could you make for a client in the same way, and how long would it take?

## 4. The command centre, UNIVERSO and Gravity

1. Where did the idea of the team as a universe come from?
2. What did you ask your dashboard agent for, and what did you decide yourself?
3. Which part do people react to most when you show it?
4. Who uses Gravity now, beyond the team site and the Lisbon Tech Fringe?
5. What would a command centre for a client's team look like? What would it show on day one?

## 5. Web Summit for RiskMandate

1. What did you do each of the four days?
2. The outreach to 50 companies: how did you choose them, and what came of it (counts only, no names)?
3. The merch print order: what was printed, by when, and what nearly went wrong?
4. Which video worked best at the stand or online, and why?
5. Your line was "Agents with a mandate". What does it mean to you?

## 6. Your agents

1. Which of your 17 agents did the most for you, and how?
2. How do you decide what an agent may do alone and what needs your yes?
3. Every message was drafted by agents and sent by you. Why that rule?
4. What would you set up first for a client starting with agents?

## 7. Business and the rest

1. What other start-ups or businesses have you founded or worked with that the site may name?
2. What did you study or train in?
3. Anything outside work that shows who you are?
4. Anything the site should never mention?

## 8. Photos, videos and contact

1. Which photos of you, your band or your work would you like on the site? Has everyone in them agreed?
2. Which videos (live music, other productions)?
3. Which contact details: LinkedIn, an email for projects, Instagram, a booking form?
4. A short showreel: would you like one cut from your work for the top of the site?

## What happens with the answers

- Your AI keeps `interview-notes.md` for you; it does not go into the repository unless you say so.
- Each change is shown to you first, then made, checked and published.
- New facts cite "Silvio, in his own words, <date>" or a link.
- Nothing about another person or company goes on the site without their agreement.
