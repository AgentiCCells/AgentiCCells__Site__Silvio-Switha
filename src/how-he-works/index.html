How he works

A producer's way of working with AI

Silvio has spent twenty years as a musician and producer. He works with AI the way a producer works in the studio.

  • 1. Vision

    He starts with a clear idea of the result. For the Know the Reach video, that was about 2,300 words of his direction.

  • 2. Immediate feedback

    He looks at what comes back straight away and says what to change, as he would at a mixing desk.

  • 3. Many variations

    He asks for many versions. The first 53 seconds of the video drew on 43 generated images, 16 motion clips and 21 edited exports.

  • 4. Choosing

    He picks what works and keeps it. The choices are his.

Numbers from newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/know-the-reach.md. In the words of Dinis Cruz's article of 15 November 2026, AI "changed the speed, not the vision".

A team of agents, with a mandate

Silvio runs his own team of AI agents. When the sources were read on 16 November 2026, it had 17 agents, 13 of them running, each with its own job:

conductor, dashboard, design, dev, video, instagram, outreach, inbox, CRM, procurement, local, vault, GAMMA-Ray scans and more.

320 commits on the team's site are his team's. For Web Summit, his agents built his command centre, placed the merch print order through the procurement agent, ran the video pipeline to YouTube, Instagram and LinkedIn, and prepared outreach to 50 target companies in the team's CRM.

Sources: web-summit.sgit.ai/people/silvio, web-summit.sgit.ai/team/silvio, ten-days-at-web-summit.md.
