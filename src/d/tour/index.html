  3. 16 motion clips, 108.7 seconds of motion
  4. 21 exports edited
  5. 1,236 credits of paid generation, kept low by
  6. 49 images reused, and one eight-second clip tested first

03

The crew

His road crew is 17 AI agents, 13 of them running, each with one job and a pass that says what it may touch.P Every message is drafted by agents and sent by him. His brief on the team site: "Silvio's project-management workflows are some of the best around."B

A producer's way of working: he sets the vision, gets fast feedback, hears many variations and chooses. The conductor runs the crew and keeps the records; the inbox agent is the only one with his mailbox, and it reads and drafts but never sends.P
