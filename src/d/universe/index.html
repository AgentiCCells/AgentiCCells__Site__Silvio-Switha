World 01 · the command centre

UNIVERSO: his team, drawn as a universe

For Web Summit, Silvio directed his dashboard agent to build a command centre. Its first world is UNIVERSO: people are planets, their agents are moons, messages are flows, and agents without a behaviour policy are flagged.P

Around it: tabs for X-Ray, Gamma-Ray, Swarm, videos, PrompTugal.news, the Lisbon Tech Fringe and the storm; tasks, workstreams, decisions, agents, Ask Claude and voice. Dark and light, phone and desktop.P

World 02 · the search

The X-Ray: what a company's agents can reach

A live search that maps what a company's AI agents can reach. It is its own world beside UNIVERSO and Gravity.P

Name a company and the screen shows the run: the searches fan out, the sources are collected and sealed, and the reach is drawn as a map, with the gap between what an agent can do and what it was asked to do.
