Know the Reach: a song and its film

For RiskMandate
Released Spotify, 13 Nov 2026K
Role music and video

Brief
A song and a music video for RiskMandate, telling what it does without overclaiming: reach, mandate and the gap between them.
What he did
Music and video, by the film's own credits.F A night in Lisbon turning to morning, a software agent shown as golden light, a human reviewer. Dinis Cruz calls him "one of the best musicians I know".H
How
He directed it like a producer: about 2,300 words in 78 messages for the first part of the video. Eight decisions shaped it, among them "the song leads" (the original song stays the timing reference) and "accurate claims". Four font families were tested on a phone screen; Inter SemiBold won.K

03 / Process

How I work: a producer's way

Five steps, our summary of how he made the film. The quoted words are Dinis Cruz's.H

  1. 01

    Vision

    "AI changed the speed, not the vision."

    He starts from what the work should feel like, with twenty years of music behind it.H

  2. 02

    Direction

    "The chorus, the structure, the pacing: those are creative decisions, and they are his."

    About 2,300 words of direction in 78 messages.K

  3. 03

    Fast variations

    "dozens, if not hundreds, of variations"

    Working "like a producer with immediate feedback", he "stayed in flow".H

  4. 04

    Choosing

    Decisions written down: "the song leads", "accurate claims". Four font families tried; one won. One eight-second clip tested before spending more.K

  5. 05

    Shipping

    Released, live, posted. Agents draft; he reviews and sends every message himself.N

"Every hour he spent carried twenty years of experience."Dinis Cruz, Every hour is twenty years, 15 Nov 2026H
